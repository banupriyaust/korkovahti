    About the data and methods

    Sources. Monthly Euribor averages, the deposit facility rate and the yield curve: ECB Data Portal. All of it is published by the ECB for free reuse; this page does not show daily Euribor fixings. Euribor is administered by the European Money Markets Institute (EMMI).

    How the outlook is built. Forward rates are computed from the ECB's AAA government-bond yield curve (Svensson model) and shifted by the gap between that curve and Euribor, measured over the latest full month. The same method gives the "today" estimate, which can differ from the official fixing by up to about a tenth of a point. It approximates what Euribor futures price; it is not a forecast by this site. The outlook stops at two years: further out the path carries the extra yield investors ask for holding longer bonds, and in back-testing since 2004 it did worse than simply assuming no change.

    Loan maths. Annuity loan (annuiteettilaina): the payment is recalculated at each reset from the remaining balance and term. Your bank's rounding, fees, interest caps and repayment type can change the result.
